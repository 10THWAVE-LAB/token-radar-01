The model, step by step

Implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −50…+150%. Maturity is 0.50 + 0.50 × (1 − e−age/365), so a protocol that has run longer with no token recorded sits closer to its full base.

    How the radar works

    1. Scope. 758 protocols at or above $5M TVL, as of the snapshot date. A protocol is pre-token when its record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol — the record may simply not carry one, so the radar says “records no token”, never “has none”.
    2. Floor. The watchlist is the 40 pre-token protocols at or above $50M TVL that are not wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that already trades. The board ranks all 40, then adds the largest pre-token protocols below the floor.
    3. Value. Base = TVL × 0.30. Growth multiplier = 1 + clamped 7-day change × 0.60, floored at 0.55. Maturity multiplier = 0.50 + 0.50 × (1 − e−age/365). No name is ever special-cased.
    4. Farm plans. Seven steps per protocol, built from its own category, chains, TVL tier and age: what to do, what it costs, how long it takes.
